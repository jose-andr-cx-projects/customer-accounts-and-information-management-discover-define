# 1D — Discovery Synthesis

**Status:** Complete enough for progression  
**Scope:** Customer Accounts + Information Management

## Customer Accounts — key learning

- duplicate and inconsistent records create uncertainty and rework;
- account records do not always map cleanly to one unique customer;
- legitimate account and relationship distinctions matter;
- staff rely on manual investigation, judgement and correction;
- duplicate signals do not prove identity;
- remediation is visible while upstream prevention is less mature;
- ownership and decision rights can be unclear;
- fragmented information and system constraints create workarounds.

### Interpretation

The problem is broader than data cleansing.

The organisation needs a clearer way to understand **what customer context is required, how trustworthy it is, and who can act on it**.

## Information Management — key learning

- the information model has been shaped strongly by Contact Centre phone-agent needs;
- content is often refined into internal handling guidance;
- Salesforce Knowledge reflects that model;
- service and knowledge relationships exist but are uneven;
- important customer-relevant information exists outside CRM;
- CX can depend on service areas manually communicating change;
- source authority, ownership and lifecycle are often difficult to see;
- informal relationships compensate for structural gaps.

### Interpretation

The problem is broader than content maintenance.

The organisation needs a clearer way to understand **what is true about a service, what has changed, and how that context becomes available wherever an interaction occurs**.

## Shared patterns

Both streams show fragmented context, manual interpretation, dependence on individual knowledge, weak ownership visibility, reactive correction and system boundaries shaping business understanding.

## Evidence maturity

Customer Accounts has stronger discovery depth.

Information Management has less complete process validation, but the systemic problem is sufficiently clear to frame and test.

Further evidence should now be gathered selectively when 3D/4D tests expose uncertainty.
