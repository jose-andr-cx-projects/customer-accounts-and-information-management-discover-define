# Purpose and Scope

**Status:** Complete enough for progression  
**Scope:** Customer Accounts + Information Management  
**Stage:** 1D Discover + 2D Define

## Purpose

Preserve the minimum evidence and problem framing needed to explain why Customer Accounts and Information Management progress together into the Business Context Architecture.

## In scope

- current-state learning;
- material pain points and risks;
- systems and operating dependencies;
- ownership, trust and lifecycle issues;
- combined problem framing;
- assumptions and evidence limitations;
- the handoff into 3D + 4D.

## Out of scope

- future-state architecture design;
- technology selection;
- implementation planning;
- approved operational policy;
- storing customer data;
- completing every SOP or process map.

## Evidence rule

Keep separate:

**evidence → reported practice → interpretation → problem frame → design hypothesis**

The historical detailed discovery remains available in Git history. The active repository contains only the synthesis needed for decision-making and continuity.
