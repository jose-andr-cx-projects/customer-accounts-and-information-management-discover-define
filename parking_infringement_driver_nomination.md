## Alerts

See **Parking Infringements - Infringement Status - Available Actions** for clarification on available actions during the parking infringement notice lifecycle.

Customers should be encouraged to submit a User Statement through the online Driver Nomination service:

- [Driver nominations](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/parking-and-transport/parking/parking-fines/pages/driver-nominations.aspx)
- Short URL: [melbourne.vic.gov.au/drivernomination](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/drivernomination)

If a customer wants to update a nominee's details, such as an address, they must complete a new driver nomination.

### Overseas driver nominations - change from 8 July 2024

From 8 July 2024, Known User statements are not accepted when they include:

- an international address; or
- a hotel or other holiday address, because these are commercial accommodation rather than a residential address.

See [Overseas driver nominations](#submission-of-known-user-statements-for-overseas-drivers) for further detail.

### PRN or FRN received while a driver nomination is unprocessed

For parking infringement notices receiving a Penalty Reminder Notice or Final Reminder Notice where a driver nomination was submitted in Salesforce:

- Do **not** place the infringement on hold when customers call about an unprocessed nomination.
- Log an **Ask case** to the Infringement Review team.
- Do not tag officers in the case; the team will do this where appropriate.

## Service description

Do not place parking infringement notices on hold when creating Expert Enquiries. The Prosecutions team will apply a hold where needed.

For other prosecution enquiries, refer to **Parking Services - Prosecutions - General Enquiry**.

### Contesting a fine or electing court

A person who is not the registered owner must first arrange for the vehicle owner to complete a User Statement.

For a company-owned vehicle, the company director or an authorised corporate officer should complete the statement.

### Hire vehicles

A hire-car driver does not need to be nominated by the hire company before objecting to a parking infringement notice.

Customer Experience Officers may advise the driver to:

1. submit an online infringement review; and
2. explain in the submission that they were driving a hire vehicle.

The Infringement Review team will assess the objection and respond.

If a Penalty Reminder Notice is issued to the hire company, the company will nominate the driver.

A hire-car driver who wants the matter heard in court must be formally nominated so that they have the required legal standing.

### What happens after a valid driver nomination

After the owner submits a correctly completed Driver Nomination, the City of Melbourne will:

1. send the owner a withdrawal letter confirming that liability has transferred; and
2. send a new copy of the fine to the nominated driver.

After receiving the new fine, the nominated driver may take action, such as applying for internal review.

### Identity theft, duplicate plates or stolen vehicles

For identity theft, duplicate registration plates, stolen plates or stolen vehicles, refer to **Parking Services - Prosecutions - General Enquiry**.

### Customers who cannot use the online form

The paper User Statement, document number `12631359`, should only be provided when the customer explains why they cannot use the online form.

Example: a government or charity organisation without an ACN may need to complete the PDF and email it to:

`prosecutions@melbourne.vic.gov.au`

## Frequently asked question

### Why does the City of Melbourne not accept an ABN for an online nomination?

An ABN may be used across different companies, whereas an ACN identifies one company.

The article states that, under the *Road Safety Act 1986*, an ACN is required as essential information for a valid nomination so the legally responsible business can be identified.

## Submission of Known User statements for overseas drivers

### Change effective 8 July 2024

The article explains that section 84BE of the *Road Safety Act 1986* permits a Known User statement to transfer liability for an infringement offence.

For an individual, the statement must include the person's full name and current home address.

The article defines a home address as the person's residential address or place of abode in Australia.

Accordingly, from 8 July 2024, Known User statements are not accepted if they contain:

- an international address; or
- a hotel or other holiday address.

The article also notes that vehicle rental and leasing companies may remain responsible under the Operator Onus provisions while recovering the fine and administration costs under their rental agreement.

Further enquiries may be sent to:

`prosecutions@melbourne.vic.gov.au`

**Ask type for international-address enquiries:** Parking - Prosecution Enquiry - Expert Enquiry

## Customer contact procedure

For questions about Driver Nominations, refer customers to the FAQ on the Driver Nomination webpage.

When a customer follows up on the progress, status or receipt of a nomination, use the following steps.

### Step 1 - Confirm submission method

Ask whether the User Statement was submitted:

- online; or
- by mail.

### Step 2 - Check the relevant record or address

#### Online submission

Ask for the Salesforce reference number and check the Salesforce case.

- **Closed case:** the nomination has been processed.
- **Open case:** the nomination is still waiting to be processed.

#### Postal submission

Confirm the address used.

The correct postal address is:

> City of Melbourne  
> GPO Box 780  
> Melbourne VIC 3001

### Step 3 - Confirm the submission date

The article states that the processing time is **8 working days**, based on the date received by the Prosecutions Compliance Team.

#### Submitted more than 8 working days ago

The article advises that the nomination may not have been received.

Ask the customer to submit another nomination through the [online service](https://www.melbourne.vic.gov.au/drivernomination). The online process provides a reference number.

#### Submitted less than 8 working days ago

Advise the customer that forms are being processed and that they will receive a letter after processing is complete.

Escalations should be submitted as Ask enquiries to the current nominated escalation contact listed in Salesforce.

## Work area

The Prosecutions Compliance Team processes User Statements for City of Melbourne parking infringements.

The process allows liability for an infringement to transfer to another person before the infringement is registered with Fines Victoria.

## Statutory declaration nominations

Driver Nominations received as a statutory declaration are legally accepted.

They may be sent as a **Nomination Ask Expert** enquiry.

Customers should also be reminded that future nominations can be submitted using the online form.
