## Purpose

This repository is the completed **Discover + Define evidence base** for two related CX problems:

- **Customer Accounts** — how the City understands and maintains customer and relationship context.
- **Information Management** — how the City understands, maintains and responds to service and operational context.

The detailed SOP and process mapping used during discovery has been synthesised into one current-state summary.

The repository now keeps only what is needed to explain:

1. what we learned;
2. how the two problems are framed;
3. why they progress together into the Business Context Architecture.

## Core conclusion

> **CX lacks a coherent way to connect trusted customer context with trusted service and operational context at the point where a customer interaction occurs.**

## Shared How Might We

> **How might we connect the business context behind customers, services and operational changes so the right context can be recognised, trusted and used wherever a customer interaction occurs?**
